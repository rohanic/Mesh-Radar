Delete your data

Last updated 23 October 2026

Mesh Radar has no accounts, so almost everything lives on your phone and you can delete it yourself in one step, even with no internet.

1. Delete everything on your phone

  1. Open Mesh Radar and go to Me.
  2. Open Privacy, then Delete everything.
  3. Confirm. Your identity key, profile, emergency card, groups, messages, photos, breadcrumbs, memories and settings are deleted at once.

Uninstalling the app also removes this data from your phone.

Messages and positions already delivered to other people's phones stay there until their owners delete them or they expire. Relayed copies expire on their own: chat after 6 hours, SOS and last-location beacons after 24 hours.

2. Data on our servers

DataHow it is deleted
Family Circle packets (encrypted)Deleted automatically after 24 hours. Turn Family Circle off to stop sending them.
Event community postsDeleted automatically after 30 days. Ask us to remove one sooner.
Purchase records (Play purchase token and your app's public key)Kept while the purchase is valid, so the licence can be restored. Ask us to delete them; the plan then cannot be restored.
First-pilgrimage pass record (a one-way hash)Ask us to delete it.
Optional analyticsTurn analytics off in the app; queued events and the random ID are deleted on the phone. Ask us to delete events already sent.

3. Make a request

Email creaeza7@gmail.com with the subject "Mesh Radar data request". Because there are no accounts, tell us what to look for:

  • for purchases, the Google Play order number from your receipt email (it starts with GPA.);
  • for a community post, the event and the text of the post.

We reply within 30 days. We never ask for your password, payment card or identity documents.

Mesh Radar helps groups stay connected. It is not an emergency service and does not replace calling one.
