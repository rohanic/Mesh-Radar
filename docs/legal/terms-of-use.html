Safety notice

Mesh Radar is not an emergency service and does not replace calling one. It helps your group stay connected, but it cannot guarantee that a message, position or SOS will arrive. Delivery depends on other phones being nearby with the app running, on Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and location being on, on battery, and on the surroundings. Distances and directions are estimates.

If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number. The emergency numbers in the app are collected from official public sources and checked before release, but numbers change; when you can, confirm them with local authorities or your event organiser.

3. How the mesh works

  • Your phone relays encrypted packets for other app users nearby while the mesh is running. Relaying uses some battery and radio time. You can stop the mesh or turn relaying off at any time, and you can choose a power mode.
  • Relayed packets cannot be read by your phone or ours. Relaying for others is free and always will be.
  • The mesh runs only after you start it and while its notification is showing. After a restart or an update you need to start it again.
  • Some phone makers stop background apps to save battery. Follow the in-app "keep running" guide for your phone.
