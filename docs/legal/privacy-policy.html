1. Who we are

Mesh Radar is published by [Publisher legal name], [country] ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Mesh Radar Android app and the small online service it uses for purchases, event pack updates, Family Circle and event communities.

2. The short version

  • No account, phone number or email address is needed.
  • Your location, messages, photos and SOS travel from phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, encrypted end to end. Phones that relay them cannot read them, and neither can we.
  • We never sell or share your data for advertising. The app has no ads.
  • Analytics and crash reports are off until you turn them on, and never include your location.
  • Delete everything works offline and removes your data from your phone at once.

3. What stays on your phone

On first launch the app creates a private identity key on your phone. It is protected by the Android Keystore and never leaves the phone. Your profile (name, avatar or photo, colour), your emergency card (blood group, conditions, medicines, home contact, hotel or tent number), your groups, messages, meeting points, breadcrumbs, settings and memories are stored on your phone.

App backups are turned off, so this data is not copied to cloud backups.

4. What goes to other phones

  • Your groups. When you join a group, your profile, emergency card, position, messages, photos and SOS go to the members of that group, encrypted end to end with a key that is shared only in person (by QR code or a code approved on the leader's screen).
  • Relaying. Other Mesh Radar phones nearby may pass your encrypted packets along. They see only a small header with no names, no location and no content. Group identifiers in that header change every 15 minutes, and device names change every hour.
  • Public SOS (optional). If you send an SOS to everyone nearby, people around you see only a rough direction and an approximate distance, not your name or exact location.
  • Event channel and crowd reports. Posts are public to app users nearby. They are signed with a different anonymous name at each event, so they cannot be linked to you or your group.
  • Lock screen. If you turn on "Show on lock screen", your emergency card appears in a notification that anyone holding your phone can read. It is off by default.

Data already delivered to other phones stays on those phones until their owners delete it or it expires (chat messages expire from relays after 6 hours, SOS and last-location beacons after 24 hours).

6. Optional analytics and crash reports

Both are off until you turn them on in the app (Me, Privacy), and you can turn them off at any time.

  • Analytics are anonymous counts, for example "a pack was downloaded" or "a regroup finished". They use a random ID that is not linked to your identity key, never include location, and are sent to PostHog. Turning analytics off deletes the queued events and the random ID.
  • Crash reports help us fix bugs. They contain technical details about the crash and your device model, without personal information, and are sent to Sentry.

7. Google Play and other services

  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive only the purchase token and order status needed to issue your licence, not your payment details.
  • Updates and ratings. The app may show Google Play's own update screen and rating dialog. Your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.
  • Google Play services on your phone provide the nearby connections and location the mesh uses. Their use is covered by Google's privacy policy.

8. Permissions

Each permission is explained in the app before it is requested.

  • Nearby devices and Bluetooth: to find and talk to nearby phones.
  • Location: to show your group on the radar. It is used only while the mesh is running, which you start yourself and which shows a notification. The app does not request background location permission.
  • Notifications: for messages, SOS alerts and the mesh status.
  • Camera: to scan group and lost-child QR codes, and to take a profile or moment photo if you choose.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): so an incoming SOS can ring through.
  • Wallpaper (optional): only when a guardian sets a lost-child card as the lock screen.

9. Children

Mesh Radar is for people aged 13 and over. A parent or guardian can add a child to a family group; the child does not create an account. The lost-child card shows only what the guardian chooses. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app without a guardian, contact us. Our child safety standards are published on a separate page.

10. Security

Messages and positions are signed and encrypted with well-reviewed libsodium primitives (Ed25519 and XChaCha20-Poly1305). Licences and event pack updates are signed so the app can check them offline. No system is perfectly secure; please protect your phone with a screen lock, because an unlocked phone shows your groups.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on your phone at any time: Me, Privacy, Delete everything. It works offline and is instant.
  • Leave a group, stop the mesh, or turn off Family Circle, analytics and crash reports at any time.
  • To ask about or delete data held on our servers, see Delete your data or email us. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Where these laws apply, we process data to provide the service you asked for (contract), for purchases and fraud prevention (legitimate interests and legal obligations), and for analytics and crash reports only with your consent.
