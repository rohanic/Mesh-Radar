Child safety standards

Last updated 23 October 2026

Mesh Radar is built to keep families, including children, together in crowds. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in every part of the app.

1. Our standards

  • Any content or behaviour that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers a child is forbidden, as is any attempt to locate or contact a child for those purposes.
  • Children never create accounts. A parent or guardian adds a child to a family group in person, chooses the child's role, and controls what the lost-child card shows.
  • Groups are joined only in person, by scanning a QR code or by a code that the group leader approves on their own screen, so strangers cannot add themselves to a family group remotely.
  • Strangers nearby never see a child's exact location. A public SOS shows only a rough direction and distance.

2. How this applies to each feature

  • Group chats are end-to-end encrypted between people who met in person. We cannot read them. Leaders can remove members, and anyone can leave a group.
  • Event communities are text only, with no photos, links or locations. Every post can be reported in the app, and a post reported by several people is hidden until we review it.
  • The event channel is rate limited and text only, and phones stop relaying keys that break the rules.

3. Reporting

Report a community post with the report button on the post. For anything else, including concerns about a group or a person, email [contact email] with the subject "Child safety report". We review child safety reports as a priority.

If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number or the police first.

4. What we do

  • We remove CSAM and other violating content we become aware of from our services and block the keys involved.
  • We report CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant authorities in the country concerned, as the law requires.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal process. Because group content is end-to-end encrypted and stored only on users' phones, we can usually provide only the limited data described in our Privacy Policy.
