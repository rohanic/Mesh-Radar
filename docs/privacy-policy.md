**Mesh Radar** helps your group stay connected when the network fails. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: you do not need an account, and we never receive your location or messages in a form we can read.

## No account

You do not give us your name, phone number or email. Your profile (name, picture, colour) and emergency card stay on your phone and are shared only with the groups you join.

## Location and messages

- Your location, messages, photos and SOS go from your phone to your group members' phones, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, encrypted end to end. Other phones that relay them cannot read them.
- **Family Circle (optional):** when it is on and you have internet, the same encrypted packets also pass through our server so your family can stay in touch over the internet. The server cannot read them and deletes them after 24 hours.
- **Public SOS (optional):** if you send an SOS to everyone nearby, people around you see only a rough direction and distance, not your name or exact location.
- **Event channel and crowd reports:** posts are public to app users nearby and use a different anonymous name at each event.

## What reaches our servers

| What | When | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Play purchase token and your app's public key | When you buy or restore a plan | To check the purchase with Google Play and issue your licence | As long as the purchase exists |
| A one-way hash of your app's public key | When you claim the free first-pilgrimage pass | So each person can claim it once | Kept |
| Encrypted packets | Only with Family Circle on | To relay them to your family | 24 hours |
| Event community posts, your city if you add it, and an anonymous per-event name | Only if you join a community and post | To show posts to other members | 30 days |
| Event pack downloads | When you update a pack | To send you the latest version | Not stored |

## Optional analytics and crash reports

Both are off until you turn them on in Settings. Analytics are anonymous counts (for example "a pack was downloaded") and never include location. Crash reports help us fix bugs. You can turn either off at any time.

## Permissions

The app asks for Bluetooth, nearby devices, location (only while the mesh is running, never in the background), notifications, and the camera (for QR codes and photos). Each request explains why. See the in-app permission screens.

## Children

Children can be added to a family group by a parent or guardian. They do not create accounts. The lost-child card shows only what the guardian chooses.

## Deleting your data

Me, Privacy, **Delete everything** removes your keys, groups, messages and history from your phone at once, even offline. Packets already delivered to other phones stay on those phones until their owners delete them.
